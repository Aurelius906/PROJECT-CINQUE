# Study materials — educator evaluation

The four documents in this folder are the instruments and administration record for
the controlled educator study reported in Sections 4.12 to 4.14 and 5.8 to 5.11.

| File | What it is | Supports |
|---|---|---|
| `TrustStudy_1_Facilitator_Protocol.docx` | Administration script, the participant-code allocation list, the five-case answer key, and the 0–2 intervention-quality rubric with its decision rules. | 4.12, 4.13 |
| `TrustStudy_2_Booklet_GroupA.docx` | The predictions-only instrument: five case pages showing the risk band and probability only, the per-case action checklist and rating items, the five post-task scales, and three open-ended questions. | 4.9, 4.12 |
| `TrustStudy_3_Booklet_GroupB.docx` | The explanation instrument: identical to Group A's, plus the contributing-factor table and SHAP chart on each case page, and four open-ended questions. | 4.9, 4.12 |
| `TrustStudy_4_Analysis_Guide.docx` | The `responses.csv` column layout, scoring and reverse-coding rules, the choice of test, and the hypotheses as stated before analysis. | 4.10, 4.13 |

## Notes for readers

**Administration.** The booklets are the instrument as designed and printed. The study
itself was administered through an online form: participants completed it themselves,
with a facilitator issuing the participant code. The checkbox glyphs in the booklets
are therefore design artefacts, not evidence of a paper administration.

**Ethics committee name.** The protocol refers to CHRPE, the committee's former name.
It is now the Humanities and Social Sciences Research Ethics Committee (HuSSREC), which
is the name used in the manuscript and on the title page. The two refer to the same body.

**Answer key.** Section 5 of the protocol contains the per-case answer key used to score
intervention quality. It is published here deliberately: the quality scores cannot be
checked without it. Data collection is complete, so nothing is compromised by releasing it.

**Hypotheses.** The Analysis Guide states directional hypotheses for trust, competence and
autonomy. All results in the manuscript are reported two-sided regardless, which is the
conservative choice and does not rely on the direction stated here (Section 4.10).

## What is not in this folder

Raw educator responses, the open-ended coding sheet, and the intervention-quality
reconciliation record are not published. They contain participants' verbatim free text and
are bound by the consent given. The aggregate outcomes they support are in `outputs/`,
and the acceptance gate re-derives every reported figure from those.
